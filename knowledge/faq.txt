# Support FAQ

Q: What is REST API?
A: REST is an architectural style for web APIs that uses HTTP methods (GET, POST, PUT, DELETE) and resource URLs.

Q: What should I do if customer sync is completed?
A: Notify the client with a short professional update and mention any remaining open items such as Sales Order sync.

Q: How long should emails be?
A: Short for quick updates, Medium for standard business notes, Long when detail and context are required.
